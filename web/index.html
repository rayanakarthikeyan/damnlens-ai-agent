FROM DOCUMENT TO DECISION

Four steps. Full visibility.

  1. Choose a document.

    Upload a PDF or image with tables. Maximum 50 pages and 40 MB.

  2. Let the agent read it.

    Native text is extracted directly. Scans use local OCR. Enable Force OCR for a broken text layer.

  3. Review the details.

    Click a row to see its source. Open validation findings. Double-click a cell to correct it; edits are saved and checks rerun.

  4. Export with context.

    Download Excel with typed values, validation, evidence and correction history. JSON and CSV are available too.

OCR can make mistakes. Recognition scores describe model confidence, not independently measured accuracy. Always review critical figures.
